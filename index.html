About Us

Son of Heaven is a new place of power in Beijing, where the ceremonial culture of the Temple of Heaven is reinterpreted through architecture, spatial design, hospitality, and contemporary art. Here, traditions take on a new form, and the ancient spirit of the altar merges with modern comfort.

Concept

The architecture of the complex rises from the earth toward heaven, preserving its strength in marble and warmth in timber. Panoramic terraces open up views from which the spirit intercepts, and ritual intertwines with modernity, creating a new format of travel: deep, meaningful, filled with the energy of nature and the culture of old Peking.

A dream of a place where legends, nature, and imperial rites come together as one

Q
00

Do I need to book tickets in advance?

Entry is rarely sold out, but our visitor model is built on conservative indicators (limited daily slots in peak season). In addition, booking online a day ahead guarantees your preferred time.

00

Can I enter the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests?

The hall is admired from the terrace; its interior is preserved as a museum piece and can be viewed from the doorway year-round.

00

What is the best time of day to visit?

Early morning, when locals practice tai chi and the light is soft. The park's magic hour is sunrise, so arriving at opening time is always rewarded.

00

Why does the Echo Wall carry whispers?

It is a matter of geometry: the perfectly circular wall reflects sound along its surface. A whisper at one end travels clearly to the other side, 65 meters away.

00

How do I get to the Temple of Heaven?

Take subway Line 5 to Tiantan Dongmen — the East Gate. Buses serve all four gates, and there are clear routes, approved signage, and partner shuttle services from major hotels.

00

Why not just see the altars from outside the park?

Because it's a different experience. The through-ticket provides: access to all three altar complexes, the Echo Wall, and the museum halls. From outside you see barely 20— 30% of the site.

00

Who looks after the site?

Management is carried out by a professional heritage administration with experience in preserving world monuments. This is the key difference from an ordinary city park.

00

How long does a visit take?

Plan around three hours, in proportion to your curiosity. The first ceremonies of the day begin at sunrise in the cypress groves.

00

Is it possible to visit in winter?

Yes, and every season has its own mood: snow on blue tiles, crisp air, and far fewer crowds.
